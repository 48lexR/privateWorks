Chapter One: New Tethori
---
So that's where I grew up: New Tethori. 

I was a good little refugee, and I went to class and learned Tethori pretty quickly. I was quiet in school for the most part, but I did make a couple friends. Didn't play any sports. Didn't do any clubs. I was just happy to be there. Hated my science classes. Truth be told, I think it's because of the whole 'AI killed my dad' thing.

Mom cried a lot those first few years. She tought I couldn't hear her, but obviously I could. She'd break down in the bathroom, in her bedroom, right as she was about to come in from outside and give me a hug. So much crying. I can't imagine how much water she must've lost.

I was so young when I lost dad that I basically don't remember him. I have no idea what he looked like, really. It's kinda haunting, sometimes. I see him in my dreams sometimes, but it's not him because I can't see his face. He haunts my dreams, so to speak.

By the time I was in high school, though, mom had mostly moved on. She was so tired all of the time doing god knows what. She'd never talk about her job. As much as I poked and prodded, she wouldn't budge. "I like it and that's all that matters," she lied. The bags under her eyes told a different story, but I never pushed it beyond that.

The fact of the matter is that we *did* have money. Sometimes it seemed like there were entire weeks when we didn't cook any food at all. We ate all sorts of good food and drank good wine while I was in high school, and I can't ever remember mom complaining that something cost too much. Once, I totaled my car on the backroads outside the city, and mom couldn't give two shits. I had another car later that week. Used, admittedly, but still another car.

But mom was kinda distant. Once I was old enough to drive myself to school, she stopped driving me there. We'd eat dinner together, but she wouldn't talk about herself at all. Just ask me questions and grill me about my grades. I deserved that, though, I was a bad student. Couldn't sit still and my mind was always wandering.

This day, though, was the day everything changed. I came home from a final exam and pulled off a sticky note from the fridge: 

*Working late. Be home around 11 pm. Hope your finals went well. -Mom*
