And there it was, sprawled out across the valley for miles. And as the sunset cast a purple glow across the sky, I couldn't help but sit down and just stare at it, slackjawed. One by one, little, golden lights shone up at us from the city as the flowers in the hills were hidden in the darkness.

"It's even more beautiful in person." Elvia breathed, seated beside me.

"I'm just surprised it's real." I sighed, laying back in the grass. "I can't believe we did it."
