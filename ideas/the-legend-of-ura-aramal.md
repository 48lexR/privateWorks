# The Legend of Ura Aramal

Since many of you may not know about Ura Aramal, let's go over it briefly.

Ura Aramal is a legendary city. The _Ura_ part means city. _Aramal_ means forgotten. Of course, I didn't know it's true name when I started this journey. We only called it _Ura Aramal_ because it was the city in the forgotten lands (called the _Aramal_.)  

Obviously, for most of my life, I didn't believe it was real. The _Aramal_ was just a legend told by adults in the central regions to scare children into eating their vegetables (a universal problem, as it seems.) The stories about it were wild. Many believed strange kind of people lived there who had pale skin and only came out at night. Some stories said that their technology was far beyond any that we had here in the Center, and that they never died because their medicine was so effective. Some believed the gods actually lived in the _Aramal_, and that _Ura Aramal_ was just a place they would congregate and decide what to do with us humans.

I didn't think I'd ever end up there, but this is the story of how I did.
